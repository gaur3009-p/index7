Our Process

From Soil to Soul

Every step of our process is designed to preserve the natural potency and purity of our turmeric.

01

Sustainable Farming

We use organic compost, crop rotation, and natural pest control methods passed down through generations. Our farms are free from synthetic fertilizers and pesticides.

02

Hand Harvesting

At peak maturity, our farmers carefully dig out each rhizome by hand to prevent damage. This labor-intensive process ensures only the finest turmeric makes it to your home.

03

Sun Drying

We dry our turmeric under the warm Haryana sun for 10-15 days. This traditional method preserves the natural oils and curcumin better than artificial drying.

04

Stone Grinding

We use traditional stone grinders that keep the temperature low, preserving the essential oils and vibrant color that make our turmeric truly special.
